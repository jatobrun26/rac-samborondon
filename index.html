01 · The country

Ecuador at a glance

A small country on the Pacific coast of South America, crossed by the equator and home to some of the most varied landscapes on the planet.

QuitoCapital city, high in the Andes
18.5MPeople (2025 estimate)
24Provinces, including the Galápagos
US$Currency: the US dollar
EspañolOfficial language: Spanish

Guayaquil is the largest city, with about 3.1 million people. Ecuador's main exports are oil, shrimp, bananas, fish and gold.

02 · Four worlds

One country, four worlds

In a single day you can go from the beach to a volcano. Ecuador is usually described as four natural regions.

La Costa

The Coast

Beaches, banana plantations and shrimp farms. Home to Guayaquil and Samborondón.

La Sierra

The Andes

Snow-capped volcanoes, Quito and colonial cities along the mountain range.

El Oriente

The Amazon

Tropical rainforest in the east, rich in wildlife and Indigenous communities.

Las Islas

Galápagos

Volcanic islands about 1,000 km offshore, famous for wildlife found nowhere else.

03 · Our home

Welcome to Samborondón

A riverside city in Guayas province, just across the bridge from Guayaquil. It has grown into one of the busiest places in the country for education, healthcare and culture.

Location
Guayas province, on the coast · 2°S, 79°W
Founded
1776 · became a canton in 1955
Population
About 82,000 residents (2022 census)
The name
From Saint Brendan, San Borondón in Spanish
Landmarks
Parque Histórico, Teatro Sánchez Aguilar and several universities
